## 📱 **How to Install as PWA**

### **On iPhone:**
1. Open Safari: https://loupecare.onrender.com
2. Tap Share button (bottom middle)
3. Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add" in top-right
5. App icon appears on home screen

### **On Android:**
1. Open Chrome: https://loupecare.onrender.com
2. Tap menu (⋮) in top-right
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. App icon appears on home screen

### **Benefits:**
- Full-screen mode (no browser UI)
- Appears in app switcher
- Push notification capable
- Offline functionality
- Fast launch from home screen

### **Common Questions to Prepare For:**

**Q: Is this connected to real smart lockers?**
A: Not yet. This is a proof-of-concept showing the complete user experience. Real IoT integration would be implemented with Raspberry Pi units at each campus location.

**Q: How will you handle payments?**
A: Integration with Malaysian DuitNow/FPX gateway for instant bank transfers, popular with students.

**Q: What's your customer acquisition strategy?**
A: Campus ambassadors (Year 4-5 students), social media (TikTok demos), and partnerships with dental school administration.

**Q: How will you scale?**
A: Start with IMU (home campus), then expand to UM, UKM, UiTM. Each campus requires 1 locker unit (~RM 1,250) and can serve 100+ students.

**Q: What's your competitive moat?**
A: First-mover advantage in Malaysia, exclusive campus partnerships, MOH-compliant processes, and zero-downtime guarantee.
